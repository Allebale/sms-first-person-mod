## First Person 1.3.0

**Co-op first person needs the launcher's Beta build of the game.** Everything else works on any game with mod support.

### New
- **Split-screen co-op:** each player has their own first-person view. Each one switches it with their own controller's button (the keyboard's V and the mouse are player 1's), and their view, aim, held FLUDD and crosshair are their own, in their part of the screen.
- **Settings preset row:** Recommended (every default) or Custom. Choosing Recommended puts every option back to its default; changing any option shows Custom.

### Works with
- **FLUDD Weapons 1.3.0** on the Beta build: the weapons are held in your view, the Rifle's zoom and scope work, and the crosshair hides with the scope.
- Playing alone, nothing changes from 1.2.3.

**Install:** unzip into the game's `mods` folder.
